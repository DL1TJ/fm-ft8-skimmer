FT8 through an FM handheld

FM-FT8 is standard FT8 sent through ordinary FM radios, with 144.575 MHz, FM, simplex as the calling frequency. This receiver listens there and shows every FT8 message it decodes, so you can check whether your signal gets out.

About FM-FT8 and how to set up your station →

Receive only. FM range is local: expect to be heard only from the area around the receiver. All times are UTC. The SNR is measured in the demodulated audio, so it is not comparable with SSB reports; "RF" is the strength of the FM carrier over the noise floor.

Every station heard here is also reported to PSK Reporter: reports of the last 24 h on pskreporter.info ↗
